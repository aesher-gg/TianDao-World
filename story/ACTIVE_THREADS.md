# Active Story Threads

> Persistent tracker for unresolved story obligations, quests, conflicts, promises, contracts, investigations, and other threads that may affect future gameplay.

## Active Threads
- None recorded.

## Rules
- Add a thread only after it is established by valid gameplay, Canon/Admin event, or another authoritative source.
- Record status, involved Character/NPC/faction IDs when known, origin, and relevant deadline/time when known.
- Remove/close a thread only when its resolution is established; preserve the resolved record in the relevant Character History or World State when materially important.
- Do not invent missing deadlines, NPC intentions, or outcomes.
